# 🛒 Olist E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze Olist e-commerce sales, customers, products, orders, payments, states, and delivery performance.

This project demonstrates my skills in **Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, and Data Visualization**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard consists of **5 interactive pages**:

### 📌 Page 1 – Olist Sales Overview

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 States by Sales
- Orders by Status
- Payment Method Distribution
- Sales by Product Category
- Average Delivery Time
- Interactive Filters

---

### 📌 Page 2 – Olist Customer Insights

- Total Customers
- Repeat Customers
- Average Orders per Customer
- Average Spend per Customer
- Customer Growth Trend
- Customer Type Distribution
- Top 5 States by Average Customer Spend
- Top 10 Cities by Customers
- Customer Distribution by State
- Average Customer Spend Trend
- Geographic Customer Analysis
- Interactive Filters

---

### 📌 Page 3 – Olist Product Analysis

- Total Products
- Average Product Price
- Average Product Weight
- Total Product Categories
- Top 10 Categories by Orders
- Top 10 Categories by Average Price
- Top 10 Categories by Sales
- Top 10 Categories by Average Weight
- Product Category Comparison
- Product Performance Analysis

---

### 📌 Page 4 – Olist Orders & Delivery

- Total Orders
- Delivered Orders
- Cancelled Orders
- Average Delivery Days
- Orders by Year
- Estimated vs Actual Delivery Time
- Orders by Status

- Average Delivery Time by Year
- Order Fulfillment Analysis
- Delivery Performance Analysis

---

- Total Customers

- Orders per Customer

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Order Status
- Order Dates
- Delivery Dates
- Product Categories
- Customer States
- Product Price
- Product Weight
- Payment Methods

- Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist
